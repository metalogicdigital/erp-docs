============
Case studies
============

Each case study describes a real customer problem, the capabilities we added on top of standard
Odoo to solve it, and how the solution is run. Customers are anonymised; the capabilities are
real. New industries are added as projects are delivered.

.. cards::

   .. card:: Retail: multi-store fashion and lifestyle chain
      :target: case_studies/retail
      :tag: Odoo 17
      :large:

      Piece-level merchandising, price-band GST, MBQ planning, a guarded goods receipt,
      inter-company distribution, a promotion studio, fast offline-capable tills, live HO ↔ store
      sync and real-time analytics for a chain of about fifty stores.
